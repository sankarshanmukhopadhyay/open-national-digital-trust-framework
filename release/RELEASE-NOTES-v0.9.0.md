# ONDTF v0.9.0 — Candidate Specification

**Release date:** 14 August 2026

## Release claim

v0.9.0 freezes the ONDTF candidate normative architecture for final implementation and governance review. The release makes the normative surface explicit, gives every candidate requirement stable conformance coverage, separates conformance classes and profile claims, introduces bounded interoperability and recognition models, hardens Guided Framework Construction, and activates candidate change control.

## Material additions

- candidate normative-document manifest and identifier stability controls;
- 28/28 normative requirement coverage through stable `CT-CAN-*` assertions;
- six named `CCL-*` conformance classes and prohibition on unqualified “ONDTF compliant” claims;
- `IPR-001` five-layer interoperability profile and deterministic cross-codebase matrix;
- `REC-001` bounded recognition profile with `EQV-*` dimension-by-dimension equivalence;
- `CAL-001` provisional calibration baseline with explicit confidence and recalibration triggers;
- internal adversarial exercise evidence and privacy/freshness comparison;
- candidate review protocol, errata/emergency process, normative freeze and v1.0.0 entrance criteria;
- versioned Guided Construction question set, migration record, and second construction package;
- expanded controlled identifier registry and candidate documentation links.

## Evidence boundary

Candidate status means stable and reviewable, not externally certified. The repository still does not claim competent legal approval for the India profile, an independently operated external implementation, production security testing, or legally effective recognition. These are retained as explicit candidate limitations and v1.0.0 entrance evidence where relevant.
