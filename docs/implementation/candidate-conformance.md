# Candidate conformance classes

The v0.9.0 candidate prohibits an unqualified claim of “ONDTF compliant”. A claim identifies a conformance class (`CCL-001` through `CCL-006`), applicable profile and version, suite version, assessed object or implementation, and assessment basis.

Every one of the 28 candidate normative requirements maps to at least one stable `CT-CAN-*` assertion in `conformance/candidate-assertions.yaml`. Machine execution is used where behaviour is deterministic; institutional and rights requirements remain assessor-verifiable or judgement-dependent rather than being falsely reduced to unit tests.
