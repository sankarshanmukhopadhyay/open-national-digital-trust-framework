# ONDTF Controlled Vocabulary

The ONDTF Controlled Vocabulary is the authoritative terminology layer for the framework. Each term is maintained as a structured YAML record, validated by the repository, and published here for readers. External glossaries, including the CTWG Glossary, inform mappings but do not control ONDTF meaning.

## Governance contract

- **Authority:** ONDTF approves and versions its own definitions.
- **Scope:** a definition is authoritative within ONDTF unless a document explicitly narrows it.
- **Evidence:** every term records ownership, lifecycle status, source context, relationships, and external mappings.
- **Change control:** normative definition changes require semantic review and release classification.
- **Revocation:** deprecated or retired terms remain traceable and identify a replacement where applicable.
