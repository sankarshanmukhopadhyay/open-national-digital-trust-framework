# External Vocabulary Mappings

Mappings support interoperability without transferring semantic authority. ONDTF records a relationship as `equivalent`, `adapted`, `broader-than`, `narrower-than`, `related`, `conflicting`, or `not-mapped`.

## CTWG Glossary

The CTWG Glossary informed the structured-record, lifecycle, provenance, cross-reference, validation, and generated-publication design. CTWG terms are mapped informatively and pinned to a reviewed release. ONDTF definitions remain authoritative for ONDTF specifications, profiles, and conformance.

The machine-readable crosswalk is maintained at `model/terminology/external-crosswalks/ctwg.yaml`.
