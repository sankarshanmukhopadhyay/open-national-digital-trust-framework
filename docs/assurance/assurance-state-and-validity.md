# Assurance state and validity

An assurance conclusion is bounded by scope, evidence cut-off, validity period and reassessment triggers. State changes require accountable authority, a reason code, effective time and evidence.

Material incidents, status changes, policy changes, ownership changes, critical vulnerabilities, expired evidence or sustained threshold breaches MUST trigger degradation, suspension, withdrawal or reassessment as specified by the profile.
