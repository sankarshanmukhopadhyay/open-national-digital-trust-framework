# External assurance mappings

External designations retain the scope, version, evaluation basis and limitations of their source scheme. Mapping does not create equivalence, certification, accreditation or legal recognition.

| Source | ONDTF treatment | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| NIST SP 800-63-4 IAL, AAL and FAL | Bounded identity mapping | Does not establish authority, delegation, execution or remedy |
| NIST CSF 2.0 | Risk and profile design pattern | Tiers are not ONDTF assurance results |
| NIST AI RMF | Govern–Map–Measure–Manage design pattern | No automatic level conversion |
| eIDAS low, substantial and high | Informative identity mapping | Does not establish transaction authority |
| FIDO certification levels | Component evidence | Not whole-service assurance |
| ISO/IEC 17000 series | Evaluation and conformity-assessment foundation | Does not itself prove control effectiveness |

The authoritative mapping records are in `model/assurance/external-assurance-mappings.yaml`.
