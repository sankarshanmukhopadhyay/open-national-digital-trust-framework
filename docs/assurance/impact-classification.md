# Decision impact classification

A profile selects DIC-0 through DIC-3 from a documented harm analysis. The analysis covers affected parties, financial and legal consequences, access to essential services, privacy and rights impacts, safety, reversibility, concentration and systemic propagation.

The impact class determines the minimum assurance design process. It does not prove that controls are effective and it is not an assurance result.
