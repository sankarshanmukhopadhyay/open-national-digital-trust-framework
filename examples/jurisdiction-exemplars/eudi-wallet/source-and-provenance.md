## Source policy

External facts in this worked exemplar are grounded in the authoritative sources below as observed by the source cut-off date. This worked exemplar is not legal advice, scheme guidance, accreditation, certification, or governmental endorsement. ONDTF modelling choices are explicitly analytical and must not be represented as the external authority's own terminology or legal interpretation.

## Provenance classes

- `source-fact` — directly supported by an authoritative source.
- `ondtf-mapping` — ONDTF interpretation of how a sourced external capability relates to ONDTF.
- `analytical-inference` — modelled state, transition, scenario or control introduced to make governance consequences testable.

## Review obligations

Before operational reuse:

1. refresh every source and effective-date claim;
2. obtain competent jurisdiction/legal or scheme review where the mapping could affect rights or obligations;
3. confirm that provider/service/status registers and certification/accreditation state have not changed;
4. review external dependency versions under `ONDTF-CON-003` and `ONDTF-MNT-002`;
5. retain unresolved differences rather than converting them into silent assumptions.
